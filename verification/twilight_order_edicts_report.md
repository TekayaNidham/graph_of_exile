- Whether Acts 5-6 / Oriath content has been released by Nov 2026 was not confirmed (wiki Act 5/6 pages are stubs); no Act 5 Twilight Order members added.
- Identity of The Saviour and Lumerius' role unknown. Torvian's priests (Vel Anxa, Herius, Cnaeus) only appear on the member list.
- Quest-log step numbers come from a commented block on the wiki page.
- Templar/Twilight Order lineage is unsupported (kept low-confidence association).
