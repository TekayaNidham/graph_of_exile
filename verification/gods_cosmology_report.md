# gods_cosmology verification report

Checked against local poewiki corpus (God, Sin, Karui Gods, Hinekora, Viridi, Three Sisters, Kitava, Torments, Kalandra, Orbala, god boss pages, Strange Voice, Lightless, Molten One, First Ones, Act 10) and the local poe2wiki subset. poe2wiki.net raw fetches were blocked (Anubis), so several PoE2 claims (Halani, Hooded One, Bodach, Kulemak) were only cross-checked through web search summaries.

## Changes
- Ralakesh: boss is in Act 7 (not Act 9). Sin drains and kills him after the Exile kills Greust. Fixed node, Exile edge (defeats kept, confidence medium), added Sin kills Ralakesh.
- Exile vs Kitava: Act 5 is a scripted loss, the real kill is Act 10. Description rewritten.
- Deleted unsupported edge faction_eternal_empire opposes Kitava (carvings only blame them for inviting him).
- Kulemak serves Master Below All: not supported (Lightless page only says the Well arose after the Master raged). Changed to associated_with, inferred, low; Kulemak summary fixed.
- Orbala = Garukhan is stated by the Orbala/Garukhan pages; edge upgraded to high, added src_poewiki_orbala. Orbala node kept medium (relic link only suggested).
- Misattributed sources: Ramako/Sione/Lani/Arohongui myths come from the God page, not Karui Gods; Tukohama/Valako wounds on Kitava from God page and Torments. Relocated.
- src_ggg_poe2_return_of_the_ancients was a third-party site (timesaver.gg); renamed src_other_timesaver_bodach.
- Karui worships Kitava downgraded to medium with explanation. Torments marked as optional quest, torments->Kitava link medium.
- Pantheon: all Pantheon edges high; concept lists major (Brine King, Arakaali, Solaris, Lunaris) vs minor gods; Pantheon->Sin edge reversed to Sin associated_with Pantheon.
- Gruthkul: noted conflicting wiki accounts (Arakaali vs Vaal). Lani Lua/Sione sun/moon wiki inconsistency noted.
- Solaris/Lunaris games reduced to poe1 (no PoE2 mention found locally).
- Hooded One reveal timing clarified (end of Act 2). Innocence summary: Avarius vessel, Bannon resurrection.
- Added nodes: deity_the_spirit, deity_goddess_of_justice, deity_god_of_domination, deity_thruldana, char_bannon; edges Innocence possesses Avarius, Bannon resurrects Innocence, Karui worships Valako/Tasalio/Tawhoa/Ramako, Azmeri worships the Spirit.

## Verified without change
Sin/Innocence twins and Original Sin (relics + Kitava video), Sin-Garukhan-Shakari family, Arakaali capturing Sin, Hinekora lore, Viridi, Three Sisters cultures, Tangmazu (Strange Voice page), Chayula/Xesht, Yugul, Brine King, Kalandra, Molten One, First Ones.

## Remaining doubts
- PoE2 pages (Halani/Azarian parentage, Bodach as 0.5 Ritual pinnacle, Kulemak details) not directly re-read; Bodach relies on tertiary guides.
- Games flags for Hinekora/Tukohama/Valako rely on partial local PoE2 corpus; Vaal Oversoul nature remains inferred/low.
- Still not covered: Innocence/Pantheon/Tasalio/Tawhoa pages themselves, Ignagduk, PoE2 druid gods.
